Thaiko

[tie-koh]nounest. 2026

1. Thai street food, cooked loud and served fast.

2. A street kitchen built like the night stalls of Bangkok: one wok, one charcoal grill, one mortar and pestle, and a queue that's always worth joining. Shared tables, cold drinks, and food that lands the moment it's ready.

See also: late-night noodles, second helpings, "just one more skewer", aroi mak.

How the night works

House rules.

  1. 01

    Order at the counter

    Tell us what you're craving and how hot you like it. We'll call your number.

  2. 02

    Sit anywhere

    Small group? No booking needed. If a seat's free, it's yours.

  3. 03

    Spice is a conversation

    Mild, medium or Thai hot. Not sure? Ask for a taste first.

  4. 04

    Share everything

    Dishes arrive as they're cooked, not all at once. Pass them around.
