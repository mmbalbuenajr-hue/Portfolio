04 — Support

Before you
take off.

Still deciding? These are the questions we hear most. Anything else, ask us when you reserve and a real person will reply within a working day.

When does AERIS ONE ship?

First units ship in March 2027, in the order reservations were made. We'll email you a delivery window two weeks before your unit leaves the warehouse, and you can cancel at any point before then.

Do I need to register it?

At 249 g, AERIS ONE sits in the lightest drone category in most countries, which often means no registration for recreational flying. Rules still vary by country and by how you fly, so check with your aviation authority, such as the FAA in the US or CAAS in Singapore, before your first flight.

What's in the box?

The aircraft, one Intelligent Flight Battery, the RC-1 screen controller, three pairs of spare propellers, a gimbal protector and a USB-C cable. The Fly More kit adds two batteries, a three-way charging hub and a shoulder bag.

What does the warranty cover?

Twelve months on the aircraft, controller and battery against manufacturing defects. Add AERIS Care for $79 and you also get one crash replacement in the first year, flyaway cover included.

What do you do with my email?

We use it to hold your place in line and send launch updates, nothing else. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe, and you can ask us to delete your details at any time.
